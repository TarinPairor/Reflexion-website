# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary website audience is adult-child caregivers of ageing parents. The primary product user is the older adult or ageing loved one. Families are evaluating whether Reflexion can support everyday independence, connection and caregiver awareness without making home feel monitored.

## Product Purpose

Reflexion is a consumer-wellness and caregiver-support system. The Reflexion Mirror supports a warm daily cognitive and wellbeing check-in, open companionship, gentle routine support and reciprocal family communication. The Reflexion Caregiver App turns permitted context into human action and family connection.

## Positioning

Reflexion joins an older adult’s active everyday interaction with meaningful caregiver awareness and a closed care loop: the caregiver can send text, voice and photos; the loved one receives them through Reflexion and may reply by voice; the relationship continues.

## Operating Context

Reflexion is designed for ordinary home routines. The first conversation each morning is a structured cognitive and wellbeing check-in that should feel like natural conversation rather than a test. Free companionship may continue afterwards. The website’s main conversion is the Get Reflexion commercial-intent flow.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Current primary product form: 21.5-inch Reflexion Mirror.
- Central caregiver surface: Reflexion Caregiver App.
- Loved-one App is a functional alternative whose public availability is QA-gated.
- Bear is a prototype; Home Hub is a concept; Tabletop Companion is a future concept.
- Reflexion does not diagnose dementia, provide clinical screening, replace professional assessment, provide emergency monitoring or guarantee safety.
- Routine completion is self-reported; the website must not imply verified adherence.
- Camera messaging is not public website content.
- Public product UI must not invent clinical scores, diagnostic labels or unsupported actions.

## Brand Commitments

Reflexion’s visual thesis is Quiet Presence: warm, premium, domestic, human, editorial, calm and trustworthy. Public founder titles are Kong Kei-Lyn — Co-Founder and Chloe Tan — Co-Founder. The permanent information architecture is Home, How It Works, Products, About, FAQ and Get Reflexion.

## Evidence on Hand

Canonical content, architecture, product truth and asset permissions live under `docs/reflexion-handoff/`. Real family imagery and real Mirror geometry are available, but asset-level consent and context rules continue to apply. Recognition wording is verified; recognition does not imply clinical validity, endorsement, sales or product-market fit. Exact permissioned human quotations are not available in the active package and must not be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Preserve older-adult dignity and active participation.
- Convert awareness into human connection, not passive monitoring.
- Keep product maturity and evidence status explicit.
- Prefer interpretable, non-clinical context over scores or alarmist claims.
- Collect only the minimum commercial-intent data needed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Mobile is a first-class target. Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA, semantic structure, keyboard usability, visible focus, reduced-motion support, readable live text and English plus Simplified Chinese architecture. Mandarin copy requires manual QA before launch.
